About Us

Built on Trust, Driven by Expertise

We bring a unique combination of deep domain expertise, personal commitment, and unwavering integrity to every engagement.

The Firm

Siddhant Yenare & Co. is a professionally managed Chartered Accountancy firm registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). Based in Ambernath, Maharashtra, we provide comprehensive financial and advisory services to individuals, SMEs, startups, and corporate entities.

Our approach combines deep technical knowledge with a client-first philosophy, ensuring every solution is practical, compliant, and aligned with your financial goals.

The Proprietor

CA Siddhant Yenare is the founding proprietor of the firm. A qualified Chartered Accountant, he brings a wealth of expertise in taxation, auditing, and financial advisory. His vision is to make professional CA services accessible and reliable for businesses of all sizes.

Our Mission

To empower businesses and individuals with expert financial guidance, ensuring compliance, growth, and financial well-being. We aim to be the most trusted financial partner for every client we serve.

Our Services

Comprehensive Financial Solutions

From tax compliance to business strategy, we offer end-to-end financial services designed to fuel your growth.

Income Tax Services

ITR filing for individuals, HUFs, firms & companies. Tax planning, assessment representation, and appeal matters handled with precision.

  • ITR Filing & E-filing
  • Tax Planning & Advisory
  • TDS/TCS Compliance
  • Assessment & Appeals

GST Services

End-to-end GST compliance including registrations, monthly/quarterly return filing, refunds, and annual reconciliation.

  • GST Registration
  • GSTR-1, 3B, 9, 9C Filing
  • E-way Bill Compliance
  • GST Refund & Audit

Audit & Assurance

Statutory, tax, and internal audits conducted with thoroughness and adherence to ICAI standards.

  • Statutory Audit
  • Tax Audit (44AB)
  • Internal Audit
  • Bank Audit

Accounting & Bookkeeping

Accurate, technology-driven accounting services that keep your books clean and compliance-ready year-round.

  • Tally / Busy Accounting
  • Payroll Management
  • Bank Reconciliation
  • MIS Reports

Company & LLP Services

Incorporation, compliance, and annual filing for private limited companies, LLPs, and partnership firms.

  • Company Incorporation
  • LLP Registration
  • ROC Annual Compliance
  • Director KYC & Changes

Business Advisory

Strategic financial guidance for startups and growing businesses — from funding readiness to financial restructuring.

  • Business Plan & Projections
  • Loan Documentation
  • MSME / Startup Registration
  • Project Finance Advisory

Compliance & Registrations

Hassle-free registrations and compliance management across various regulatory frameworks.

  • PAN / TAN Registration

  • Professional Tax

Wealth & Investment Advisory

Personalized financial planning and investment advisory to help you grow and protect your wealth.
